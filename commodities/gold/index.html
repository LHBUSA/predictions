What moves gold?

Research considerations, not a proprietary buy/sell signal or a claim about today's market direction.

Three structural drivers

  • Real yieldsHigher inflation-adjusted yields can raise the opportunity cost of holding an asset that pays no interest. The historical relationship varies by regime.
  • The dollarGold is commonly quoted in U.S. dollars. Exchange-rate moves can change demand and prices across jurisdictions.
  • Official-sector demandCentral-bank reserve decisions and investment flows can influence demand, but reported holdings may be delayed or incomplete.

How this market works

Gold is used as a reserve asset, for jewelry and in industry. Unlike a business, it generates no earnings or dividends; it also has storage, insurance and financing costs.

Where the thesis can break

A defensive reputation does not guarantee a positive return in a stock-market selloff. Gold can decline alongside equities when investors need liquidity, and prolonged increases in real yields may matter.

The investable proxy · GLD

Trust shares may reflect the underlying metal but include custody, costs, supply/demand and market mechanics. A trust share is not an ounce and trades on the U.S. equity calendar.
