METHOD

Evidence, not a black box

The market never enters the model

Kalshi prices, order books and volume are stored as a benchmark only. A code guard and a database constraint reject any market-derived feature; a test proves PBE probabilities are unchanged when every market price moves.

Model the contract, not the headline

Each market is normalized to its exact station, window, threshold and resolution authority — or held with a machine-readable reason. Chicago rain resolves at O'Hare; the Chicago high at Midway.

No retroactive rewrites

Forecasts, contracts and resolutions are append-only. Venue settlement and the independent official value are stored separately. Methodology →
