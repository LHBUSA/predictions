PropBetEdge Predictions

Predictive intelligence, built as a living ledger.

PropBetEdge Predictions is a live predictive intelligence platform. It monitors prediction markets, publishes forecasts from proprietary models, keeps every forecast in an append-only ledger and scores each one against the official outcome. By studying price movement, historical outcomes, model performance and market behavior across weather, rates, macro, crypto and equities, we build new structured datasets, find where markets and models disagree, and improve the forecasting systems behind the PropBetEdge intelligence network.

  • Live market monitoring
  • Append-only forecast ledger
  • Proprietary models
  • Scored against official outcomes
